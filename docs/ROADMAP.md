# Roadmap

Each milestone ships as one or more PRs with tests, and must beat the original game's
numbers from milestone 0 before it counts as done.

Reference source: OpenJK at `260c59c2907187af555a676fe0cc798893bf7757` (2026-09-29).
`code/` is single-player, `codemp/` multiplayer; about 1M lines of C/C++ between them.

| # | Milestone | Done when |
|---|-----------|-----------|
| 0 | **Baseline and setup** (multiplayer FPS and load time done, frame times of world rendering on the demo's camera path done, see BASELINE.md; frame-time spikes of the full game and single-player pending): Rust workspace and local CI; measure the original game's FPS, frame-time spikes and load times on fixed scenes; screenshot-comparison tool | The original's numbers are recorded in `docs/BASELINE.md` and reproducible with one script |
| 1 | **Assets** (**done**: `crates/fs`, `crates/bsp`, `crates/image`, `crates/shader`, `crates/console`): `.pk3` archives, virtual filesystem with the original's search order, `.bsp` maps, textures (TGA, JPEG, PNG), `.shader` scripts, cvars and configs | Every file in the retail `.pk3`s parses; tests pin the results |
| 2 | **Map viewer** (started: Vulkan bring-up and offscreen rendering in `crates/render`; map surfaces, patches and stitching in `crates/world`; textured, lightmapped preview render of a map to PNG; material resolution in `crates/shader/src/resolve.rs`; stage rendering, fog, sky, deforms and flares matching all 11 reference views; the original's visibility culling; the same camera path timed in both engines: with two frames in flight, 1.4-1.7x the original's frame rate offscreen; the map viewer `swjkja view`, which flies through a map in a window or exclusive fullscreen; presenting measured to the step (the compositor costs both engines about 1 ms, our copy to the window 0.6-0.8 ms, drawn straight into the image in a window), faster than the original in both: 1.04-1.08x in a window and 1.05-1.18x in fullscreen on an idle PC, 1.15-1.45x with a busy PC, see BASELINE.md (speed goal met); a loading screen with a progress bar, the first frame 0.26-0.35 s after the window opens; next: milestone 3): Vulkan renderer for world geometry, lightmaps, shader stages, fog, sky | Fly-through matches the original's screenshots; faster than the original on the same scenes |
| 3 | **Models** (started: MD3 meshes and their levels of detail in `crates/model`, every retail MD3 parsing; the light grid that lights models and the static models a map spawns in `crates/world`; the map's static models drawn in the viewer, culled and lit like the original's, matching three mp/ffa5 reference views, see BASELINE.md; single-player maps follow single-player spawn and draw rules, matching four single-player reference views; single player's breakables in their initial state, sent by the server's PVS of their box, matching a t3_stamp reference view; gun and ammo racks with their ammo and medpacks, beacons and planted bombs, an ammo rack matching the same view; Ghoul2 meshes and skeletons in `crates/model`, every retail `.glm` loading with its skeleton, and the racks' weapons drawn posed at frame 0, matching two t2_dpred reference views; Ghoul2 skins and surface switches, and single player's NPCs drawn with their skins, holding their weapons and sabers' hilts (bolted Ghoul2 models) in the stance their first movement frames give them and dropped to the floor (box traces against the map's brushes and solid entities), each part at the frame its animation reaches at the view's time (the idle animation they play after five seconds standing still, ghoul2's frame timing and lerp), matching three reference views; next: NPCs animating in the viewer as time passes): Ghoul2 (`.glm`/`.gla`), MD3, skins, sabers and effects | Characters animate and match the original |
| 4 | **Single-player**: collision, movement, entities, ICARUS scripts, NPC AI, sound, UI, saves | The campaign is playable start to finish |
| 5 | **Multiplayer**: new protocol, server, client, bots | Matches play on Windows and Linux |
| 6 | **Mods**: asset mods load; Rust mod interface for code mods | Popular asset mods load unchanged |
